## Users

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Every claim of skill points to a project, repo, or role the visitor can check.
2. **Two audiences, one fast path each.** A recruiter reaches experience and the resume, and a client reaches shipped work and contact, within seconds and without detours.
3. **Show full-stack range.** Projects make both sides visible: the interface and the system behind it.
4. **Contact is always one step away.** Every page has a clear next action.
5. **The site is itself a work sample.** Performance, accessibility, and build quality are part of the portfolio's argument.
